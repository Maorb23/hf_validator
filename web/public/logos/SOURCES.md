# Technology strip asset sources

Downloaded from official sources for the local design prototypes. Marks retain their original colors and proportions.

- NVIDIA: the green eye path from the official website header at https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/legal-info/logo-brand-usage/; used alongside Nemotron, TensorRT-LLM, and Dynamo labels. No separate product logos are claimed.
- SGLang: official organization avatar, https://github.com/sgl-project.png?size=96 (organization: https://github.com/sgl-project).
- vLLM: compact vector from https://github.com/vllm-project/media-kit/blob/main/vLLM-Logo.svg.
- Nebius: unmodified white and black outline variants from the official media kit at https://nebius.com/media-kit; archive https://assets.nebius.com/assets/2dd2ad13-45c1-4429-a47c-156bedd4d71d/NEBIUS-logos%2807.25%29%20%281%29.zip.
- LLM Optimizer: a custom chart symbol from this prototype's existing icon set, not an official external logo.

Technology identification does not imply sponsorship or universal runtime compatibility.
